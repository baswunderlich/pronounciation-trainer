Sarah woke up early, her stomach fluttering with excitement and nervousness. Today was her first day at a brand-new school, and she wasn’t sure what to expect. She carefully put on the outfit she had picked out the night before and double-checked her backpack to make sure she had everything—pencils, notebooks, and the little stuffed bunny keychain her mom had given her for luck.

At breakfast, her mom smiled reassuringly. "You're going to do great, sweetheart," she said. Her dad gave her a thumbs-up. "Just be yourself."

As they pulled up in front of the school, Sarah took a deep breath. The building looked so big, and groups of students were already chatting and laughing together. Would she make friends? Would she like her teacher?

The classroom was bright and welcoming, with colorful posters on the walls. Her teacher, Mrs. Carter, greeted her warmly. "Welcome, Sarah! We're so happy to have you here." Sarah smiled shyly and found her desk.

Throughout the day, things started to feel a little easier. She met a girl named Emma, who sat next to her and showed her where to find the crayons during art class. At lunch, a group of kids invited her to sit with them, and by recess, she was playing tag on the playground, laughing as she ran.

When the final bell rang, Sarah couldn’t believe how quickly the day had passed. She ran to meet her mom at the school gate, her nervousness from the morning completely gone.

"How was it?" her mom asked.

Sarah grinned. "It was amazing! I think I made some new friends."

As they walked home, she told her mom all about her first day, feeling happy and excited for the days to come.
